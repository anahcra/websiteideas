Raja Labs · Nanoscale Spectroscopy

Seeing excitons.

A research group at the Molecular Foundry, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. We image and time-resolve excitations in two-dimensional materials with light and electrons, down to the nanometer and femtosecond.

Research

Light and electrons, one layer thick.

01
Ultrasmall imaging

Correlated optical and low-loss electron energy-loss spectroscopy to image excitons inside a single moiré unit cell, at sub-nanometer resolution.

02
Ultrafast dynamics

Ultrafast electron diffraction to watch energy and charge move across 2D heterostructure interfaces, including phonon-mediated transport unique to these systems.

03
Dielectric screening

Tuning the bandgap and excitons of a 2D semiconductor through its environment, and the disorder that local dielectric fluctuations create.

04
Moiré potentials

Painting potential landscapes on an atomically thin canvas: how twist and reconstruction localize, delocalize, and transport interlayer excitons.
